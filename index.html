Where to Start

Three kinds of website

Not every business needs the same thing. Here are the three ways I build sites — from a simple one-pager to a full dynamic platform. Pick the one that fits where you are now; you can always grow into the next.

1

One-Page Static Site

Simple, fast, all in one place

Everything lives on a single page in one file — your story, services, and contact info, all in one smooth scroll. The fastest and most affordable way to get a real presence online.

  • Single page, single file
  • Loads almost instantly
  • Mobile-friendly by default
  • Contact details & a simple form
  • Cheap, simple hosting
Best for New businesses, freelancers, or anyone who just needs a clean, professional landing page.

2

Multi-Page Static Site

More room, more features

Everything in tier one, spread across multiple pages — plus the extras. Separate pages for services, gallery, or menu, and add-ons like automated contact emails and other small functions layered on top.

  • Multiple pages of content
  • Automated email on form submit
  • Photo gallery, menu, or services pages
  • Optional extra functions & add-ons
  • Still fast, still affordable to host
Best for Established local businesses that need to show more — and want a few smart, time-saving extras.
Start Here
3

Dynamic Site · Firebase

All the bells and whistles
Full Power

A proper, fully dynamic website powered by Firebase. Content that updates itself, customer accounts, online booking, databases, and live data — the kind of site that runs part of your business instead of just describing it.

  • Database-driven, dynamic content
  • Customer logins & accounts
  • Online booking or ordering
  • Admin dashboard — update it yourself
  • Scales as your business grows
Best for Businesses that need their website to actually do things — bookings, accounts, and more.

Why not just use Wix or Squarespace?

DIY builders look easy but the costs and trade-offs add up fast. Here's the real comparison over 3 years.

Wix Core $1,044+ over 3 years $29/mo × 36 months — plus you're stuck on Wix forever, ads on lower tiers, limited SEO control, and the site looks like a template.
Squarespace Business $828+ over 3 years $23/mo × 36 months — beautiful templates but they all look the same, transaction fees on sales, and you can't export your site if you leave.
Johann (Multi-Page Static) $300–350 + $10/mo $300–350 one time + $10/mo hosting ≈ $660 over 3 years — and because most of the cost is upfront, it only gets cheaper the longer you keep it: the gap vs. Wix and Squarespace widens every year while they keep charging full price. Custom-built, you own every file, no platform lock-in, no transaction fees, and a site that actually looks like yours.

And it's future-proof. DIY builders lock you into whatever platform and technology they choose — if they change direction, hike prices, or you outgrow them, you're stuck rebuilding from scratch. A site hand-built on plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript runs on the open web standards every developer knows, so you stay agile: it's easy to redesign, extend, or build on top of whenever your business changes.
